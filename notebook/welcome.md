# Welcome to your SSH notebook 👋

This is your **universal clipboard**, served over SSH.

- Use `↑`/`↓` to move around the file list
- Press `enter` to open a note
- Press `q` or `esc` to go back
- Press `/` to filter files

## What's next

This is **v0.1** — read-only. Coming up:

1. Real auth (password + your SSH keys)

> Tip: you can deep-link straight to a file, e.g. `ssh localhost -p 2222 notes/ideas.md`
